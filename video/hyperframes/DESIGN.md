# AMD Physical AI Demo Film

## Style Prompt

Editorial technical cinema: the evidence is the hero, with quiet confidence, precise typography, restrained motion, and a warm human rhythm between real robot footage and clear engineering diagrams. The visual language should feel like a research lab shipping a reliable system, not a generic AI trailer. Use dark graphite scenes for motion and evidence, bright paper scenes for explanation, and the existing Datawhale red as the publication accent.

## Colors

- `#0b0d10` — cinematic canvas and video framing
- `#14171b` — graphite panels and evidence background
- `#f4f5f7` — paper background and readable title scenes
- `#dc2738` — Datawhale accent, active markers, and key calls to action
- `#36b8a7` — verified success and AMD execution evidence
- `#c27611` — protocol, success-seed, and boundary markers

## Typography

- Primary: `Inter`, then system sans-serif
- Data and command lines: `ui-monospace`, `SFMono-Regular`, `Menlo`, monospace
- Use large titles sparingly; keep technical labels compact and readable
- Letter spacing is `0`; never use condensed or novelty display fonts

## Motion

- Use 30 fps and frame-accurate timing.
- Prefer calm crossfades, masked reveals, short horizontal moves, and deliberate scale-ins.
- Use red only for the active idea; use teal for verified outcomes and amber for protocol scope.
- Transitions should expose the relationship between scenes: clip to metric, metric to architecture, architecture to clip.
- Keep the actual robot motion untouched; animate only the evidence layer around it.

## Composition Rules

- 16:9 master at 1920x1080; keep every important label inside a 120px safe margin.
- Use one primary video frame with a small evidence rail, never a wall of cards.
- Multi-view layouts are reserved for DISCOVERSE and RoboCasa camera evidence.
- Every score overlay includes model, task, seed, protocol, and result class.
- Keep successful cases before the compact boundary-case chapter.

## Delivery Requirements

- Use archived robot footage for every task scene.
- Label each formal score with its protocol and denominator.
- Use restrained transitions and a compact evidence overlay.
- Keep the success montage tied to published result records.
- Place captions inside the title-safe area and away from contact points.
